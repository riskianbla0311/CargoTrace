CARGOTRACE AUTH PAGE VISUAL REFINEMENT

This patch only replaces:
- resources/views/auth/login.blade.php
- resources/views/auth/register.blade.php
- public/images/cargotrace-auth-illustration.png

Install:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Copy resources and public folders into C:\laragon\www\CargoTrace-Complete and choose Replace.
3. Run: php artisan optimize:clear
4. Refresh /login (Ctrl+F5).

This patch does not replace routes/web.php or AuthController.php; keep the working register routes and controller already installed.
